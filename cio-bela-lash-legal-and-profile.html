Document 01

Shared Lash Station Agreement

The Collaborative Vision

We are building the lash department of Cio Bela together. This agreement is a living document intended to ensure that our shared station remains professional, hygienic, and inspiring for every artist who works within it. As we grow, we will refine these standards as a team.

1. Professionalism & Aesthetic

  • Station Presentation: Each lash artist is responsible for keeping their designated area organized, clean, and visually aligned with the salon's brand between and after all appointments.
  • Atmosphere: We agree to maintain a professional tone and a welcoming, private environment for all clients.
  • Personal Branding: You are encouraged to market your unique style and approach; however, any permanent changes to the physical suite must be discussed with the collective.
  • Client Privacy: The lash station is an intimate service environment. We commit to ensuring client privacy and dignity at all times — including proper draping, controlled suite access during services, and discretion in all client communications.

2. Shared Station Stewardship

  • Common Areas: The restroom and waiting area are shared. We agree to "leave it better than we found it" to ensure a high-end experience for every guest.
  • Adhesive Residue & Cleanliness: Adhesive residue on surfaces or trays must be addressed immediately after each client. It is each artist's responsibility to remove all adhesive drips, used micro-brushes, and debris from shared equipment before the next artist uses the station.
  • Turnover: Every lash artist is responsible for fully sanitizing the lash bed, tray, and surrounding surfaces between every appointment. The suite must be left in ready-to-use condition for any artist who follows.
  • Adhesive Care: Adhesive must be capped tightly and stored in a cool, dry, airtight container after each use to preserve shelf life and prevent fume buildup in the shared station.

3. Logistics & Access

  • Entry: Each artist will have keyed access. We agree to ensure the building is fully secured and all non-essential lights and equipment are turned off when the last person leaves.
  • Client Flow: We will respect the privacy of the floor. If a client arrives early, we will guide them to the designated waiting area — not the lash station — until the artist is ready to receive them.
  • Guest Policy: Lash artists must communicate their individual policies regarding children or extra guests to their clients in advance. Due to the intimate nature of lash services, additional guests are generally discouraged at the station during treatments.
  • Friends & Family: To maintain a focused, professional environment, friends and family members of lash artists are not permitted to occupy the salon unless they are present to receive a service.

4. Supplies & Inventory

  • Storage: Each artist is allocated their own designated space for personal supply storage. Items left on shared surfaces or in common areas may be moved to ensure suite safety and cleanliness.
  • Shared Equipment: Any shared warming units, trolleys, or suite furniture must be treated with care and returned to their designated positions after each use.
  • Disposables: Each artist is responsible for managing their own gel pads, micro-brushes, tape, and disposable liners. Shared laundry facilities are available for headrest linens; please move loads promptly.

5. Conflict & Resolution

Because we are building this together, we agree to open and honest communication. Any friction regarding suite scheduling, supplies, noise, or shared space will be handled privately and professionally between the parties involved, or brought to management for a group discussion.

6. Cancellations & Calling Out

As an independent business owner, you are fully responsible for managing your own schedule and client communications. If you are unable to come in, it is your sole responsibility to contact your clients directly and in a timely manner to inform them and arrange rescheduling. The salon is not responsible for notifying your clients on your behalf. Consistent failure to handle cancellations and rescheduling in a professional and timely manner reflects on the salon as a whole. The salon reserves the right to terminate your rental agreement if this standard is not upheld on a consistent basis.

7. Texas Compliance (TDLR)

We collectively agree to keep the suite "Inspection Ready" at all times. Please ensure a copy of your current license and a government-issued photo ID are available for inspection at all times. Proper labeling of "Clean" versus "Dirty" implements and strict adherence to single-use policies are non-negotiable standards in our shared environment.

Document 02

Daily Stewardship Checklist

Opening Procedures — "The Set Up for Success"

  • Lights & Atmosphere: Turn on all suite lighting. Ensure the music or ambient playlist is at an appropriate, relaxing volume.
  • Adhesive Prep: Open a fresh adhesive dot and confirm it is within the correct temperature and humidity range for optimal bonding before your first appointment.
  • Station Prep: Verify the lash bed is fully sanitized, fitted with a clean liner, and all supplies (gel pads, tape, lash trays, tweezers) are stocked and within reach.
  • Waiting Area Check: Straighten any shared seating or décor. Ensure the check-in area is tidy and welcoming.
  • Restroom Check: Verify it is clean, stocked with toilet paper and hand towels, and the trash is empty.

Between Every Client — "The Turnover"

  • Station Reset: Remove and dispose of all used gel pads, tape, and micro-brushes immediately. Replace with fresh disposables before the next client.
  • Surface Sanitation: Wipe down the lash bed, trolley, and any shared surfaces with an EPA-registered, hospital-grade disinfectant after every single appointment.
  • Adhesive Residue Removal: Clear all adhesive residue from the lash tile, jade stone, and immediate work area. Do not leave residue for the next artist.
  • Implements: Remove all used or contaminated tools from the work surface immediately. Single-use items go directly in the lined waste bin — never set aside for reuse.
  • Hand Hygiene: Wash hands thoroughly with soap and water or use 70% alcohol-based sanitizer before receiving the next client.

Throughout the Day — "The Flow"

  • Waste Management: Empty your personal station waste bin before it becomes full. Double-bag any adhesive-saturated or bodily-fluid-contaminated waste.
  • Adhesive Monitoring: Replace the adhesive dot every 15–20 minutes throughout the day to maintain proper bonding strength and prevent using adhesive past its working window.
  • Linen Rotation: If a load of headrest covers is dry, fold them and put them away; move the next load to the dryer.

Closing Procedures — "The Clean Slate"

  • Adhesive Shutdown: Cap all adhesive bottles tightly and store in a cool, dry, airtight container.
  • Full Surface Wipe-Down: Perform a final sanitation of the lash bed, trolley, and all hard surfaces touched during the day.
  • Waste Disposal: Empty the station waste bin into the main dumpster. Replace with a fresh liner.
  • Linen Check: Confirm all soiled headrest covers are in the laundry. No used linens should remain at the station overnight.
  • Supplies Stocked: Restock any low supplies (gel pads, tape, micro-brushes, adhesive) so the station is ready for the next artist who opens.
  • Final Walkthrough: Lights off, station is clean, adhesive is capped, and the front door is locked.
Document 03

Texas Safety & Compliance Protocols

1. Licensing & ID (Texas Law)

  • Visibility: Your current Texas Cosmetology or Esthetician license must be displayed at your workstation in public view at all times.
  • Identification: You must have a government-issued photo ID available at the salon for inspection by TDLR at any time.
  • Updates: You are responsible for notifying the Owner of any license renewals to keep our master Salon Renter List current.

2. Client Intake & Contraindications

  • Pre-Service Consultation: Every new client must complete a client intake form prior to their first service. This form must document known adhesive sensitivities, current medications, eye conditions, and any known contraindications.
  • Contraindication Screening: You are responsible for refusing or modifying service for any client presenting with contraindications including, but not limited to: known adhesive/cyanoacrylate allergies, active eye infections, recent eye surgery, or any condition affecting the eye area.
  • Patch Tests: A patch test is strongly recommended for all new clients and any client who has changed their skincare routine, medications, or reported new sensitivities since their last visit.

3. Infection Control & Sanitation

  • Hand Hygiene: Wash hands with soap and water or use a 70% alcohol-based sanitizer before and after every client, without exception.
  • Surface Disinfection: All non-porous surfaces — including the lash bed, trolley, and tray — must be cleaned and disinfected with an EPA-registered, hospital-grade disinfectant between every client.
  • Single-Use Strict Policy: All gel pads, micro-brushes, and tape are strictly single-use and must be discarded immediately after one application. Reusing single-use adhesive applicators between clients is a TDLR violation and a contamination risk.
  • Headrest Coverings: A fresh disposable headrest cover or a clean linen must be used for every single client. Used coverings must be removed and disposed of immediately after each appointment.
  • Isolation Standard: Every natural lash must be individually isolated prior to extension application. Lash-to-lash bonding ("stickies") is a safety violation and grounds for formal review.

4. Adhesive Application Safety

  • Dot Replacement: Adhesive dots must be replaced every 15–20 minutes during service to maintain proper bonding and prevent using adhesive past its working window.
  • Eye Protection: Under-eye gel pads must fully cover and protect the lower lash line at all times during application. Adhesive must never be applied in amounts that risk skin or eye contact.
  • Ventilation: Adequate ventilation must be maintained at your station at all times during adhesive use. N95 masks are strongly recommended.

5. Chemical Safety

  • SDS Binder: All artists must provide a Safety Data Sheet (SDS) for any professional adhesive, primer, or chemical preparations they bring into the salon.
  • Pre- and Post-Care Products: Any pre-treatment cleansers, primers, or aftercare products used on clients must be professional-grade and applied in accordance with manufacturer guidelines.
  • Storage: All products must be stored in their original containers, properly labeled, and kept away from direct heat or open flames.

6. Eye or Skin Contact Exposure Procedure

If adhesive contacts a client's eye or skin during a service, the following protocol must be followed immediately:

  1. Stop the service immediately.
  2. Flush the affected area with clean water for 15 minutes.
  3. Refer to emergency care if irritation persists.
  4. Document the incident and notify Cio Bela management immediately.
  5. Clean and disinfect all tools and surfaces before resuming service or before the next client.
